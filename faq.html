FAQ

常見問題，讓你更快找到適合的茶。

如果你正在挑選飲茶禮盒、沖泡方式或送禮組合，這裡是最實用的資訊來源。

Common questions to help you choose with confidence.

Looking for tea gifts, brewing advice, or product recommendations? These answers cover the essentials.

你們的茶葉來自哪裡？

我們主要採購台灣本土茶園，包含阿里山、高山、南投與彰化等產區，並會標註產地與採收季節。

Where do your teas come from?

We source mainly from Taiwanese tea regions including Alishan, mountain farms, Nantou, and Changhua. Each harvest includes origin and season info.

適合第一次喝茶的人怎麼選？

若想慢慢入門，先選高山茶或包種茶，口感較柔和、容易掌握，也適合搭配日常。

What should beginners try first?

For a gentle introduction, choose a high-mountain or Baozhong tea. They are smooth, approachable, and easy to brew well.

有哪些送禮方式？

我們提供預設禮盒、企業禮贈、婚禮茶禮與自定義客製化包裝服務。

Do you offer tea gifts?

Yes. We provide standard gift boxes, corporate gifts, wedding tea sets, and fully custom packaging for personal or business occasions.

茶葉可以保存多久？

常溫密封保存，通常可保持 6–12 個月的風味。避免陽光、潮濕與強烈異味。

How long does tea stay fresh?

Stored sealed and cool, tea usually stays fresh for 6–12 months. Keep it away from sunlight, moisture, and strong odors.
