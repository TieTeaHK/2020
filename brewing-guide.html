Brewing Guide

煮茶其實跟音樂一樣：講究節奏。

每一種茶都有適合的溫度、時間與器具。簡單的泡法，能讓茶葉自然開展，回甘更明顯。

Tea is like music: rhythm matters.

Every tea has a preferred temperature, steeping time, and vessel. A balanced brewing method brings out clarity, sweetness, and depth.

高山茶

水溫：80–85°C
泡法：1g/10ml，第一泡 1 分鐘，第二泡可加 15–30 秒。
風味：花香、甘甜、清爽。

High Mountain Tea

Water: 80–85°C
Method: 1g per 10ml, steep 1 minute first infusion and +15–30 seconds afterward.
Flavor: floral, sweet, bright.

烏龍茶

水溫：90–95°C
泡法：1g/8ml，前三泡各 1–2 分鐘。
風味：蜜香、厚實、清雅。

Oolong

Water: 90–95°C
Method: 1g per 8ml, steep 1–2 minutes for first three infusions.
Flavor: honeyed, layered, aromatic.

綠茶

水溫：75–80°C
泡法：1g/12ml，第一泡 45–60 秒。
風味：清亮、草本、鮮甜。

Green Tea

Water: 75–80°C
Method: 1g per 12ml, steep 45–60 seconds on first infusion.
Flavor: fresh, herbal, sweet.

紅茶／黑茶

水溫：95–100°C
泡法：1g/8ml，第一泡 2–3 分鐘。
風味：醇厚、回甘、果香。

Black Tea

Water: 95–100°C
Method: 1g per 8ml, steep 2–3 minutes on the first infusion.
Flavor: full-bodied, sweet, fruity.

Quick Tips
💧

用水很重要

優先使用軟水或過濾水，茶香更清，口感更柔和。

Quality Water

Prefer soft or filtered water for a cleaner, softer, more aromatic taste.

🫖

器具影響風味

瓷器、玻璃與紫砂各有不同的保溫與香氣釋放特點。

Vessel Matters

Porcelain, glass, and Yixing clay each warm and release aroma differently.

🌤️

慢慢泡

不要急著衝一壺，耐心讓茶葉慢慢打開，香氣與回甘自然更長。

Slow Brew

Let the leaves unfold gradually; patience gives a longer, sweeter finish.
