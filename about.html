About TieTea

讓每一泡茶都回到土地與人情。

我們相信，茶的好不好，不只是香氣，還是怎麼被種、被採、被製成，與喝茶的人如何相遇。

Every tea should reflect the land, the care, and the story behind it.

We believe that tea is not only aroma—it is how it is grown, harvested, processed, and matched to the person who drinks it.

我們的理念

起源於對台灣茶文化的深愛，我們以小農合作和慢工藝方式，保留茶葉最自然的風味與生命力，打造更貼近日常的茶飲體驗。

Our Philosophy

Founded on a love for Taiwan's tea culture, we work through small-farm partnerships and slow craftsmanship to preserve each tea's natural vitality and everyday elegance.

我們看重

風土、採摘時機、焙火節奏與使用者的感受，我們選茶不追求極端，而是追求平衡、舒適與回甘。

What Matters

We care about terroir, picking timing, roasting rhythm, and the user's experience. We favor balance, comfort, and a long finish over extremes.

從茶園到茶杯

我們與茶農建立慣常的合作模式，從採青、製茶到包裝，每一個步驟都被檢視，讓每一批茶都帶著真實的地理氣候與人情味。

From Farm to Cup

We develop enduring relationships with tea growers, monitoring each stage from plucking to processing and packaging so every batch reflects its true region and care.
