Sustainability

好茶，不能犧牲土地與下一代。

我們讓採購更透明，工藝更有記憶，盡力減少包裝浪費，讓茶葉長期與自然共存。

Good tea should not cost the earth.

We aim for transparent sourcing, respectful processing, and thoughtful packaging to keep tea in harmony with the land.

土地保護

我們優先支持以自然方式耕作、維持生態平衡的茶園，避免過度投入與破壞土壤。

Land Care

We prioritize growers who work with nature, preserve biodiversity, and maintain sustainable soil health.

減少浪費

採用可回收紙盒與低過度包裝設計，避免不必要的塑膠與紙張消耗。

Waste Reduction

We use recyclable packaging and simplified designs to reduce plastic and excess material use.

小農協作

長期合作，讓茶農獲得穩定收入，保留產地茶文化與手工技術。

Farmer Partnerships

Long-term cooperation gives growers stable income and helps preserve local tea culture and craftsmanship.

透明供應鏈

每一批茶都會公開來源地、產季與製程資訊，讓顧客更信任每一口茶。

Transparent Supply

We share origin, harvest season, and production notes so customers know exactly what they are drinking.
