Tea Story

台灣茶，不只是飲料，而是山與季節的味道。

每一片茶葉都帶著土地的溫度、雨季與日照，一杯茶，彷彿能把四季帶進杯中。

Taiwan tea is more than a beverage—it is a taste of place.

Every leaf carries weather, elevation, and a particular season. Tea becomes a way to sip the landscape itself.

山高雲靜

高海拔茶園通常茶葉香氣更細緻，回甘更明顯，帶有山林與雲霧的餘韻。

High Elevation

Mountain-grown tea tends to be more delicate and aromatic, with sweeter, cleaner finishes and the lingering feel of cool mist.

采收時節

春茶清甜、夏茶較厚、秋茶圓潤，因氣候與日照變化，每一季都帶出不同面貌。

Seasonality

Spring teas are bright and sweet; summer teas are thicker; autumn harvests tend to be rounder and more layered.

製程與風味

輕焙、重焙、日曬、半發酵，都是調整香氣與口感的關鍵。

Craft and Process

Light roast, heavy roast, sun-drying, and partial oxidation all reshape aroma, body, and aftertaste.

品飲與回憶

喝茶不只是品味，而是一種靜定，讓人慢下來，留住一段安定的時光。

Tasting and Memory

Tea is more than taste—it is a ritual of calm. It encourages time to slow down and notice the moment.
